Rules
• Roll 2 dice per turn. Double = 12 steps (6 + 6) and an extra turn
• Roll a 6 to bring a token out of the base
• Capture = extra turn. ★ spaces are safe. Capture is required to unlock the home lane
• Entering the home lane = extra turn
• If you have a chance to capture but do not take it, that token returns to start
• If all your tokens are in the home lane, roll only 1 die
• When all 4 tokens reach the center, give your 1-die number to another player
• If all 4 tokens reach the final stretch (50) without a capture, or all opponents finish, you lose without a capture
